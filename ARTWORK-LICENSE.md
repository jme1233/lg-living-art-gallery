# Artwork permissions and sources

All active images are local JPEGs. Individual source URLs, attribution, original/delivered dimensions, permissions and file hashes are recorded in [artwork-sources.json](artwork-sources.json).

Met images were downloaded only when the object API returned isPublicDomain=true and a primaryImage URL. The Met makes Open Access images available under CC0: https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access .

Bierstadt is marked free to use on its Smithsonian record: https://americanart.si.edu/artwork/among-sierra-nevada-california-2059 .

Monet is marked public domain on its National Gallery record: https://www.nga.gov/artworks/74796-japanese-footbridge .

The two forest photographs are marked public domain by the official Olympic National Park Flickr account; their source links are in the manifest. They are U.S. government works.

Moonlit Ridge, Tidal Ribbons and Quiet Horizon are original programmatic geometric studies, not museum paintings or AI-generated artwork. They and the sample generator may be used, modified and redistributed for this project and its public prototype.

Images were converted to baseline sRGB JPEG and fitted within 3840 × 2160 without cropping or upscaling. Black display margins preserve the original proportions.
